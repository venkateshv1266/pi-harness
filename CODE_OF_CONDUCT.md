# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the
maintainer at **venkateshv1266@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and
will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to respect
the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban
contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
